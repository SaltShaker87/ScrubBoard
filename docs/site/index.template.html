How to use it

  1. Copy text as usual.
  2. Click the Scrubboard icon and choose Clean my clipboard. On a Mac you can also right-click selected text and choose Services, then {{SERVICE_TITLE}}.
  3. Paste. Read the cleaned text before you send it.

{{DISCLAIMER}}
Installing on Windows: "Windows protected your PC"?

Scrubboard isn't code-signed on Windows yet, so Windows shows this warning the first time. Click More info, then Run anyway. The installer doesn't need administrator rights.

Installing on Ubuntu

Double-click the downloaded file. It opens in App Center; click Install. Then open Scrubboard from the app grid. It walks you through a short welcome the first time.

Privacy

Scrubboard never sends your text anywhere. It has no accounts and no tracking, and it doesn't keep a record of what you copy. It works without an internet connection.
